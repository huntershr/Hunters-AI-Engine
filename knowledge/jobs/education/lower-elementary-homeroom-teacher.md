# Lower Elementary Homeroom Teacher (Native English Speaker)

## Job Brief
Acts as class teacher for a lower-elementary class (about grades 1 to 3, ages 6 to 9) in an English-medium school in Egypt, teaching core subjects in English, building early literacy and numeracy and looking after each child's personal development.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach core subjects in line with the school's curriculum (for example an American or British programme)
- Teach early literacy (phonics, reading, writing) and numeracy
- Create a safe, positive and well-organised classroom environment
- Assess students, track progress and report to parents
- Differentiate tasks so that all students are supported and stretched
- Work with specialist teachers and teaching assistants
- Communicate with parents through meetings, reports and daily contact
- Take part in school events and duties and follow the school's safeguarding policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in education or a related field
- A recognised teaching qualification or licence
- At least 2 years of experience teaching lower elementary or primary students
- Native or near-native English; based in Egypt, as the post requires
- Patience, warmth and strong classroom organisation

## Required Skills
- Primary classroom teaching
- Early literacy and phonics
- Numeracy teaching
- Native-level English
- American or British curriculum knowledge
- Assessment and progress tracking
- Differentiation
- Classroom management

## Core Competencies
- Child-centred teaching
- Communication
- Classroom management
- Planning and organisation
- Collaboration
- Professional ethics and child safeguarding

## Essential (Deal Breaker)
- Primary classroom teaching
- Native-level English

## Nice to Have

- Experience in an international school
- Experience with a reading or writing programme
