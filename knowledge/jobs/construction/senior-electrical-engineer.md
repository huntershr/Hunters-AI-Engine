# Senior Electrical Engineer

## Job Brief
Senior electrical engineer who leads the electrical design or execution of building and infrastructure projects, reviews the work of other engineers, resolves technical issues with the consultant and client and answers for quality, cost and schedule on the electrical scope.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead the electrical design or execution of the project and plan resources and sequence of work
- Review calculations, drawings, shop drawings and material submittals and approve or return them with comments
- Supervise and mentor electrical engineers, supervisors and subcontractor teams
- Coordinate with the consultant, client, utility authorities and other disciplines to resolve technical issues
- Prepare and check quantities, cost estimates, variation orders and subcontractor valuations
- Plan and witness testing and commissioning, and review as-built drawings and handover documents
- Monitor progress against the programme and report delays and recovery plans to the project manager
- Make sure electrical safety, quality and environmental standards are followed

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering (power or electrical systems)
- 6–9 years of experience in electrical engineering, including leading design or site teams
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and working knowledge of DIALux, ETAP or similar design software
- Strong knowledge of the Egyptian electrical code and international standards such as IEC, BS or NEC
- Strong written and spoken English and good reporting and presentation skills
