# Arabic Teacher Y7 / الصف الأول الإعدادي

## Job Brief
Teaches Arabic language to Year 7 (first preparatory) students in an international school in Egypt, following the Ministry of Education Arabic curriculum alongside the school's own programme, and builds students' grammar, reading, writing, spelling and appreciation of Arabic literature.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and deliver Arabic lessons that follow the Ministry of Education curriculum and the school's programme
- Teach grammar and morphology (نحو وصرف) with clear explanations and regular practice
- Develop reading comprehension and fluent, expressive reading
- Develop writing and expression, including paragraphs, short compositions and creative tasks
- Teach literature and poetry and help students analyse and enjoy texts
- Teach spelling and dictation (إملاء) and correct written work with useful feedback
- Assess and track student progress, and support students who need extra help or extension
- Communicate regularly with parents and work with colleagues on planning and school events

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Arabic language or education
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching Arabic at middle school level
- Strong command of Modern Standard Arabic
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
