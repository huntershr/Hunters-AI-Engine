# English Head of Department

## Job Brief
Leads the English department of a school, setting the direction for English language and literature teaching, leading and mentoring the team, and raising standards through assessment, moderation and results analysis.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead curriculum planning and schemes of work for English language and literature
- Lead, support and mentor the English teaching team
- Carry out lesson observations and moderation of student work and assessments
- Oversee assessment and exam preparation, and keep marking and reporting consistent
- Analyse results, set department targets and plan improvements
- Maintain high teaching standards and share good practice across the department
- Communicate with parents and report to senior leadership on department progress
- Manage the department's teaching resources and teach classes as timetabled

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in English or a related field (master's degree preferred)
- A teaching qualification (PGCE, QTS or equivalent)
- At least 5 years of experience teaching English, including 2 or more years in a leadership or coordinator role
- Strong command of English
- Strong leadership, organisation and communication skills
