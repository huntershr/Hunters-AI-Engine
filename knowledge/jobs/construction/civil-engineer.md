# Civil Engineer

## Job Brief
Civil engineer who carries out site execution or design office work on building and infrastructure projects, from reading drawings and supervising works to quantity checks, quality control and progress reporting.

## Key Responsibilities
- Supervise civil works on site including excavation, foundations, concrete, masonry, waterproofing and finishing works
- Review construction drawings, specifications and method statements and make sure work follows them
- Coordinate with subcontractors, suppliers, consultant representatives and other disciplines on site
- Prepare quantity take-offs, material requests and monthly progress and quantity reports
- Carry out and document inspections, material approvals and tests with the quality team
- Resolve site technical issues and raise requests for information when drawings are unclear
- Monitor the work programme and report delays and their causes to the project manager
- Enforce health, safety and environment rules on the works under their control

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering
- 3–5 years of experience in civil engineering on site or in a design office
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and MS Office; working knowledge of Primavera P6 or MS Project
- Knowledge of the Egyptian Code of Practice (such as ECP 203 for reinforced concrete) and international standards such as ACI or BS
- Good written and spoken English; able to work on site
