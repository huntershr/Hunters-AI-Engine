# Junior Electrical Engineer

## Job Brief
Entry-level electrical engineer who supports the design or installation of power and lighting systems in buildings and projects under the supervision of senior engineers, learning drawings, load calculations, installation checks and testing.

## Key Responsibilities
- Assist in preparing electrical drawings and schedules for power, lighting and small power systems
- Help with load calculations, cable sizing and protection settings under supervision
- Check installation of conduits, cable trays, cables, panels and fittings against approved drawings
- Take measurements and prepare quantity take-offs and material requests
- Assist in testing and commissioning and record the results
- Prepare daily site reports and keep inspection and delivery records
- Follow health and safety rules and electrical work permits

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering (power or electrical systems)
- 0–2 years of experience in electrical design or installation (fresh graduates with internships or site training are welcome)
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and MS Office, especially Excel
- Basic knowledge of low-voltage electrical systems, cable sizing and the electrical codes used in Egypt
- Good written and spoken English; willing to work on site
