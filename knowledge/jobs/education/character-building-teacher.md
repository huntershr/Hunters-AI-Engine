# Character Building Teacher

## Job Brief
Teaches character and values education (such as respect, responsibility, honesty, empathy, resilience and teamwork) through discussion, stories, activities and projects, and helps build a positive and respectful school culture.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach character-building lessons that match the school's values programme and the age of the students
- Use stories, role play, discussion and group activities to teach values and life skills
- Promote positive behaviour and a respectful school culture
- Run activities and projects on citizenship, responsibility and community service
- Help students build social and emotional skills such as managing feelings, teamwork and resolving conflict
- Work with homeroom teachers, counsellors and parents to support individual students
- Observe students, record progress and share it with the school team and parents
- Follow the school's safeguarding, health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in education, psychology, social studies or a related field
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching or working with school-age children
- Strong English; Arabic is an advantage
- Patience, warmth and the ability to be a positive role model
