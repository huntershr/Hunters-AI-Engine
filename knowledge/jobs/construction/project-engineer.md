# Project Engineer

## Job Brief
Engineer who supports the project manager in delivering a construction or MEP project, coordinating site teams, subcontractors and the consultant, tracking progress, quantities and documents, and making sure the works follow the drawings, specifications and programme.

## Key Responsibilities
- Coordinate daily between site teams, subcontractors, the consultant and the client on technical and progress matters
- Track progress against the project programme and prepare weekly and monthly progress reports
- Prepare and follow up material and shop drawing submittals, requests for information and technical approvals
- Check quantities and prepare subcontractor and supplier valuations and interim payment certificates
- Prepare and follow up variation orders and change requests with the project manager
- Monitor quality, safety and environmental compliance on site and follow up corrective actions
- Keep project records up to date, including drawings, correspondence, meeting minutes and handover documents
- Support the project manager in planning resources, materials and equipment

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Civil, Mechanical or Electrical Engineering
- 3–5 years of experience as a site, project or contracts engineer on construction or MEP projects
- Proficiency in MS Project or Primavera P6, AutoCAD and MS Office
- Knowledge of construction contracts, quantity take-off and project documentation
- Good written and spoken English and strong report writing skills
