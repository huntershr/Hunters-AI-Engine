# Reading Teacher (KS2)

## Job Brief
Teaches reading to upper-primary students (about ages 7 to 11), building phonics knowledge, fluency, comprehension and vocabulary, supports students who are behind in reading and encourages a love of books.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach reading lessons, including guided reading, comprehension and vocabulary
- Assess students' reading levels and group them for targeted teaching
- Teach reading strategies such as inference, prediction and summarising
- Run intervention sessions for students who are below the expected reading level
- Build a reading-for-pleasure culture through the library, book clubs and reading challenges
- Track progress and report to parents and the school team
- Work with English and homeroom teachers to link reading with writing and other subjects
- Follow the school's safeguarding and health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in English, education or a related field
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching primary English or reading
- Strong command of English and knowledge of phonics and reading methods
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
