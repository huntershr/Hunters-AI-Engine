# English Teacher – Primary (Year 5 / KS2)

## Job Brief
Teaches English to upper-primary students (about Year 3 to Year 6, ages 7 to 11) following the school's British-style curriculum, building reading, writing, grammar, spelling and speaking skills, and tracks each child's progress against Key Stage 2 expectations.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach engaging English lessons in line with the school's primary curriculum and Key Stage 2 expectations
- Teach reading comprehension, writing, grammar, punctuation and spelling
- Develop speaking and listening through discussion, drama and presentations
- Assess student work regularly and give clear, constructive feedback
- Differentiate tasks so that all students are supported and stretched
- Keep records of progress and report to parents and the school team
- Create a positive, well-organised classroom with clear routines
- Follow the school's safeguarding and health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in English, education or a related field
- A recognised teaching qualification (PGCE, QTS or equivalent)
- At least 2 years of experience teaching English at primary level
- Strong command of English
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
