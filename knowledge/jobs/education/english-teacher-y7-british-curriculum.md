# English Teacher Y7 (British Curriculum)

## Job Brief
Teaches Key Stage 3 English to Year 7 students following the British curriculum and the Cambridge Lower Secondary / IGCSE pathway, building their reading, writing, spoken English, grammar and understanding of literature, and tracks progress against KS3 expectations while keeping lessons inclusive, well-behaved and safe.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach engaging Year 7 English lessons in line with the KS3 curriculum
- Teach reading, writing, spoken English, grammar and literature
- Assess student work against KS3 expectations and give clear, useful feedback
- Differentiate tasks so that all students are supported and stretched
- Track progress, identify students who need support and share outcomes with the team
- Promote positive behaviour for learning and a calm, respectful classroom
- Follow the school's safeguarding policies and keep students safe
- Communicate regularly with parents and work with the English department on planning

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in English or a related field
- A teaching qualification (PGCE, QTS or equivalent)
- At least 2 years of experience teaching English at secondary level
- Experience teaching the Cambridge curriculum (Cambridge Lower Secondary and/or Cambridge IGCSE English)
- Strong command of English
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
