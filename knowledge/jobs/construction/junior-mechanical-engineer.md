# Junior Mechanical Engineer

## Job Brief
Entry-level mechanical engineer who supports the design or installation of HVAC, plumbing and firefighting systems in buildings and projects under the supervision of senior engineers, learning drawings, load calculations, installation checks and testing.

## Key Responsibilities
- Assist in preparing mechanical drawings and schedules for HVAC, plumbing and firefighting systems
- Help with HVAC load calculations, duct and pipe sizing under supervision
- Check installation of ducts, pipes, equipment and fittings against approved drawings
- Take measurements and prepare quantity take-offs and material requests
- Assist in testing and commissioning such as pressure tests and balancing, and record the results
- Prepare daily site reports and keep inspection and delivery records
- Follow health and safety rules and work permits

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering
- 0–2 years of experience in mechanical design or installation (fresh graduates with internships or site training are welcome)
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and MS Office, especially Excel
- Basic knowledge of HVAC, plumbing and firefighting systems
- Good written and spoken English; willing to work on site
