# Junior Civil Engineer

## Job Brief
Entry-level civil engineer who supports site execution or design office work under the supervision of senior engineers, learning to read drawings, check quantities and follow quality and safety procedures on building and infrastructure projects.

## Key Responsibilities
- Assist in supervising civil works on site such as excavation, concrete, masonry and finishing works
- Read construction drawings and check dimensions, levels and reinforcement against the approved drawings
- Take site measurements and help prepare quantity take-offs and material requests
- Prepare daily site reports and keep records of inspections, tests and material deliveries
- Support inspections and concrete cube sampling with the quality control team
- Follow health and safety rules and report unsafe conditions to the site engineer
- Learn from senior engineers and carry out tasks assigned by the project team

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering
- 0–2 years of experience in civil or building construction (fresh graduates with internships or site training are welcome)
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and MS Office, especially Excel
- Knowledge of the Egyptian Code of Practice for reinforced concrete (ECP 203) and basic site practices
- Good written and spoken English; willing to work on site
