# Preschool English Teacher (KG1–KG2)

## Job Brief
Teaches English to preschool children (about 3 to 6 years old) through play-based, hands-on lessons that build speaking, listening, early phonics, vocabulary and pre-writing skills, and helps every child feel safe, curious and confident.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach daily English lessons through songs, stories, games, role play and hands-on activities
- Teach early phonics, letter recognition, vocabulary and simple sentences
- Use positive classroom management and keep children safe and supervised at all times
- Observe each child, record progress and share it with parents and the school team
- Work with homeroom teachers and assistants to keep routines and expectations consistent
- Prepare classroom displays, materials and learning corners that match the school's curriculum and calendar
- Take part in parent meetings, school events and staff training
- Follow the school's child protection, health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree (education, English or a related field)
- At least 1 year of experience teaching preschool or early-years children
- Strong spoken and written English
- Knowledge of early-years methods (play-based learning, phonics)
- Patience, energy, warmth and good communication with children and parents
