# Head of Arabic Department

## Job Brief
Leads the Arabic department of a school in Egypt, setting the direction for Arabic language teaching (and Arabic social studies where the school offers it) in line with the Ministry of Education curriculum, leading and mentoring the team and raising standards through assessment and results analysis.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead curriculum planning and schemes of work for Arabic in line with the Ministry of Education curriculum and the school's programme
- Lead, support and mentor the Arabic teaching team
- Carry out lesson observations and moderation of student work and assessments
- Oversee assessment and exam preparation, and keep marking and reporting consistent
- Analyse results, set department targets and plan improvements
- Maintain high teaching standards and share good practice across the department
- Communicate with parents and report to senior leadership on department progress
- Manage teaching resources and teach classes as timetabled

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Arabic language or education (master's degree preferred)
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 5 years of experience teaching Arabic, including 2 or more years in a leadership or coordinator role
- Strong command of Modern Standard Arabic
- Strong leadership, organisation and communication skills
