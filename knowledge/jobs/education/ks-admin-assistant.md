# KS Admin Assistant (Key Stage Administrative Assistant)

## Job Brief
Supports the Key Stage head and the stage office with day-to-day administration, including student records, attendance, correspondence, scheduling and communication with parents and staff.

## Key Responsibilities
- Maintain student records, attendance sheets and files accurately
- Handle correspondence, emails, phone calls and messages for the Key Stage office
- Prepare letters, circulars, reports and meeting documents
- Schedule meetings, parent appointments, events and cover arrangements
- Support the Key Stage head with timetables, trips, exams and assessment logistics
- Coordinate with teachers, reception, admissions and other departments
- Handle parent and student queries politely and pass them to the right person
- Keep confidential information secure and follow school policies

## Required Qualifications
- Diploma or bachelor's degree in business administration or a related field
- At least 1 year of administrative experience, ideally in a school
- Strong written and spoken English; Arabic is an advantage
- Proficient in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Outlook)
- Organised, discreet and reliable
