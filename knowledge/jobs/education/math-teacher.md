# Math Teacher

## Job Brief
Teaches mathematics to students at the level of the post (primary, middle or high school) following the school's curriculum, building number, algebra, geometry, data and problem-solving skills and preparing students for school assessments and external exams where offered.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach mathematics lessons in line with the school's curriculum and the level of the post
- Teach number, algebra, geometry, measurement and data handling with clear explanations and worked examples
- Build problem-solving and mathematical reasoning through tasks and discussion
- Assess student work regularly, give clear feedback and track progress
- Differentiate tasks so that all students are supported and stretched
- Prepare students for school tests and external exams or checkpoints where the school offers them
- Communicate regularly with parents and work with the maths department on planning
- Follow the school's safeguarding, health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in mathematics, a related subject or education
- A recognised teaching qualification (PGCE, QTS, teaching licence or equivalent)
- At least 2 years of experience teaching mathematics at school level
- Strong command of English
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
