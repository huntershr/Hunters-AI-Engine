# Arabic Teacher – Lower Primary

## Job Brief
Teaches Arabic to lower-primary students (about ages 6 to 9, grades 1 to 3) following the Ministry of Education curriculum and the school's programme, building letter knowledge, reading fluency, handwriting, spelling and vocabulary.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and deliver Arabic lessons that follow the Ministry of Education curriculum and the school's programme
- Teach letters, sounds, vowel marks (الحركات) and early reading
- Teach handwriting, spelling and dictation (إملاء) with regular practice
- Build vocabulary, listening and speaking through songs, stories and games
- Develop simple writing and expression
- Assess and track progress, and support children who need extra help
- Create a warm, well-organised classroom with clear routines
- Communicate regularly with parents and follow the school's safeguarding policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Arabic language or primary education
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching Arabic at primary level
- Strong command of Modern Standard Arabic
- Patience, warmth and clear communication with young children and parents
