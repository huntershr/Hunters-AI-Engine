# Academic Coordinator

## Job Brief
The Academic Coordinator supports curriculum implementation and academic quality across grade levels or subject areas, working closely with teachers and school leadership. This role monitors teaching standards, coordinates assessment schedules, and helps ensure consistent delivery of the school's academic program.

## Key Responsibilities
- Coordinate curriculum delivery and academic scheduling across grade levels or subject areas
- Monitor teaching quality through classroom observations and provide constructive feedback to teachers
- Support planning and administration of internal and external assessments
- Analyze student performance data and recommend interventions to improve academic outcomes
- Facilitate professional development sessions and collaborative planning among teaching staff
- Liaise between teachers, department heads, and school leadership on academic matters
- Ensure academic policies and procedures are consistently applied across the school

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Education or a relevant subject area; postgraduate qualification preferred
- 5+ years of teaching experience with prior coordination or leadership exposure
- Strong knowledge of curriculum standards and assessment practices
- Excellent command of English and Arabic

## Core Competencies
- Instructional leadership and mentoring
- Data-driven decision-making
- Collaborative and diplomatic communication
- Organizational and scheduling proficiency
- Commitment to academic quality and standards
