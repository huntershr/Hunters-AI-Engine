# Digital Marketing Specialist

## Job Brief
The Digital Marketing Specialist plans and executes online marketing campaigns across social media, search, and email channels to build brand awareness and drive engagement. Reporting to the Marketing Manager, this role tracks campaign performance and optimizes digital strategy based on data insights.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and execute digital marketing campaigns across social media, search, and email channels
- Create and schedule engaging content aligned with brand guidelines and campaign goals
- Monitor and analyze campaign performance using analytics tools and report on key metrics
- Manage paid advertising budgets across platforms to maximize return on investment
- Conduct keyword research and support SEO efforts to improve organic visibility
- Coordinate with design and content teams to produce marketing assets
- Stay current on digital marketing trends and recommend new tactics and platforms

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, or related field
- 2–4 years of experience in digital marketing
- Proficiency in Google Analytics, Meta Ads Manager, and SEO tools
- Strong copywriting and content creation skills

## Core Competencies
- Data-driven campaign optimization
- Creative content development
- Digital platform proficiency
- Analytical and results-oriented mindset
- Adaptability to evolving digital trends
