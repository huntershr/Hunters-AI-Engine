# German Teacher

## Job Brief
Teaches German as a foreign language to school students, from primary to secondary level as the post requires, building listening, speaking, reading, writing and grammar skills in line with the school's programme.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach German lessons for the assigned year groups in line with the school's programme and the CEFR levels
- Teach listening, speaking, reading and writing together with vocabulary and grammar
- Use songs, games, dialogues, role play and authentic materials to keep lessons engaging
- Assess students regularly, give clear feedback and prepare them for exams or certificates where the school offers them
- Differentiate tasks so that all students are supported and stretched
- Introduce students to German-speaking cultures and traditions
- Track progress, communicate with parents and work with the German department
- Follow the school's safeguarding, health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in German, modern languages or education
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching German as a foreign language at school level
- Native or near-native German (C1 or higher) and good English
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues

## Required Skills

- Assessment and progress tracking
