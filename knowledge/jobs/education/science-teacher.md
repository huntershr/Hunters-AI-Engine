# Science Teacher (KS3 / Middle School)

## Job Brief
Teaches science to middle-school students (for example Year 7 to Year 9 or KS3), covering biology, chemistry and physics through clear explanation, practical work and scientific enquiry, and keeps all laboratory work safe.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach science lessons in line with the school's curriculum (for example KS3 or Cambridge Lower Secondary)
- Teach biology, chemistry and physics concepts through explanation, demonstrations and practical work
- Plan and run safe practical experiments and enforce laboratory safety rules
- Develop scientific enquiry, data handling and investigation skills
- Assess student work, give useful feedback and track progress
- Differentiate tasks so that all students are supported and stretched
- Work with the science department and lab technician on planning and resources
- Communicate with parents and follow the school's safeguarding policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in a science subject (biology, chemistry or physics) or in science education
- A recognised teaching qualification (PGCE, QTS or equivalent)
- At least 2 years of experience teaching science at middle or secondary level
- Strong command of English
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
