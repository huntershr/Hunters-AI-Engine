# Humanities Teacher (Middle & High School)

## Job Brief
Teaches humanities (history, geography and social studies) to middle and high school students, building research, critical thinking and writing skills and helping students understand people, places and societies.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach humanities lessons in line with the school's curriculum
- Teach history, geography and social studies concepts using sources, maps and case studies
- Develop research, critical thinking and essay-writing skills
- Organise class discussions, projects and, where possible, fieldwork or trips
- Assess student work, give useful feedback and track progress
- Differentiate tasks so that all students are supported and stretched
- Contribute to department planning and share good practice
- Communicate with parents and follow the school's safeguarding policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in history, geography, social sciences or education
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching humanities at secondary level
- Strong command of English
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
