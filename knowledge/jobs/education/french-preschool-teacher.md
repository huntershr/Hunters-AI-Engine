# French Preschool Teacher (KG1–KG2)

## Job Brief
Teaches French to preschool children (about 3 to 6 years old) through play-based, hands-on lessons built on songs, rhymes and stories that develop listening, speaking, early vocabulary and simple phonemic awareness, and helps every child feel safe, curious and confident.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach daily French lessons through songs, rhymes, stories, games and role play
- Build early French vocabulary, simple phrases and listening and speaking confidence
- Develop simple phonemic awareness through sound games, rhymes and repetition
- Run classroom routines such as greetings, circle time and transitions in French
- Use positive classroom management and keep children safe and supervised at all times
- Observe each child, record progress and share it with parents and the school team
- Work with homeroom teachers and assistants to keep routines and expectations consistent
- Prepare displays, materials and learning corners, and follow the school's child protection, health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree (education, French or a related field)
- At least 1 year of experience teaching preschool or early-years children
- Native or near-native French (C1 or higher)
- Knowledge of early-years methods such as play-based learning
- Patience, energy, warmth and good communication with children and parents
