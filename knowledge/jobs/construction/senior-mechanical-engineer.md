# Senior Mechanical Engineer

## Job Brief
Senior mechanical engineer who leads the mechanical design or execution of building and infrastructure projects, reviews the work of other engineers, resolves technical issues with the consultant and client and answers for quality, cost and schedule on the mechanical scope.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead the mechanical design or execution of the project and plan resources and sequence of work
- Review calculations, drawings, shop drawings and material submittals and approve or return them with comments
- Supervise and mentor mechanical engineers, supervisors and subcontractor teams
- Coordinate with the consultant, client, civil defence and other disciplines to resolve technical issues
- Prepare and check quantities, cost estimates, variation orders and subcontractor valuations
- Plan and witness testing, balancing and commissioning, and review as-built drawings and handover documents
- Monitor progress against the programme and report delays and recovery plans to the project manager
- Make sure quality, safety and environmental standards are followed

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering
- 6–9 years of experience in mechanical engineering, including leading design or site teams
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and working knowledge of HAP, Carrier E20-II or similar design software
- Strong knowledge of international standards such as ASHRAE, SMACNA and NFPA, and Egyptian codes for mechanical works
- Strong written and spoken English and good reporting and presentation skills

## Required Skills

- HVAC, plumbing and firefighting systems

- Testing, balancing and commissioning

## Core Competencies
- Technical leadership and sound engineering judgement
- Coaching and supervising engineers and technicians
