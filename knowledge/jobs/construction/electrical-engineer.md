# Electrical Engineer

## Job Brief
Electrical engineer who designs, supervises or commissions low-voltage electrical systems for buildings and projects, including power distribution, lighting, earthing and protection, working to approved drawings and standards.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design or review low-voltage power, lighting and small power systems for buildings and projects
- Prepare load calculations, cable sizing, protection coordination and single-line diagrams
- Supervise installation of panels, cables, cable trays, lighting and earthing systems on site
- Review shop drawings, material submittals and method statements and coordinate with the consultant
- Coordinate with mechanical, civil and low-current teams to avoid clashes
- Carry out inspections, testing and commissioning and prepare completion documents
- Prepare quantity take-offs, progress reports and requests for information
- Enforce health, safety and electrical permit-to-work rules

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering (power or electrical systems)
- 3–5 years of experience in electrical design or site supervision in buildings or projects
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and MS Office; working knowledge of DIALux or similar lighting software
- Knowledge of the Egyptian electrical code and international standards such as IEC or BS 7671
- Good written and spoken English; able to work on site
