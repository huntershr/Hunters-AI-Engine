# Mechanical Engineer

## Job Brief
Mechanical engineer who designs, supervises or commissions HVAC, plumbing and firefighting systems for buildings and projects, working to approved drawings, specifications and standards.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design or review HVAC, plumbing, drainage and firefighting systems for buildings and projects
- Prepare cooling and heating load calculations, equipment selection and duct and pipe sizing
- Supervise installation of chillers, air handling units, ducts, pipes, pumps and firefighting equipment on site
- Review shop drawings, material submittals and method statements and coordinate with the consultant
- Coordinate with electrical, civil and architectural teams to avoid clashes
- Carry out inspections, testing, balancing and commissioning and prepare completion documents
- Prepare quantity take-offs, progress reports and requests for information
- Enforce health, safety and environment rules on the works under their control

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering
- 3–5 years of experience in mechanical (HVAC, plumbing, firefighting) design or site supervision
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and MS Office; working knowledge of HAP, Carrier E20-II or similar load calculation software
- Knowledge of international standards such as ASHRAE, SMACNA and NFPA, and Egyptian codes for mechanical works
- Good written and spoken English; able to work on site
