# Student Management Officer

## Job Brief
Supports student affairs by monitoring attendance and behaviour records, following up on student welfare, communicating with parents and helping the school day run smoothly.

## Key Responsibilities
- Monitor and record student attendance and punctuality and follow up on absences
- Record behaviour and discipline incidents and escalate them according to school policy
- Work with teachers, counsellors and stage leaders on student concerns
- Communicate with parents about attendance, behaviour and student matters in English and, where needed, Arabic or French
- Help supervise arrival, dismissal, breaks and school events as scheduled
- Maintain student files and reports accurately
- Help new students settle in and understand school routines
- Follow the school's safeguarding and confidentiality policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in education, social sciences, business administration or a related field
- At least 1 year of experience in student affairs, school administration or a customer-facing role
- Strong written and spoken English
- Proficient in Microsoft Office
- Calm, fair and professional manner with students and parents
