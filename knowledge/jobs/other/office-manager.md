# Office Manager

## Job Brief
The Office Manager oversees the day-to-day administrative operations of the organization, ensuring smooth office functioning, efficient use of resources, and reliable support for staff across departments. Reporting to senior management, this role coordinates facilities, vendor relationships, and administrative staff to keep the workplace organized and productive.

## Key Responsibilities
- Oversee daily administrative operations and ensure smooth office functioning across departments
- Manage office supplies, equipment, and vendor contracts to ensure cost-effective procurement
- Supervise administrative and support staff, assigning tasks and monitoring performance
- Coordinate facilities management including maintenance, security, and workplace safety
- Maintain organized filing systems and ensure proper document control and record-keeping
- Support senior management with scheduling, correspondence, and meeting coordination
- Ensure compliance with company policies and administrative procedures across the office

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration or related field
- 3–5 years of experience in office management or administrative coordination
- Proficiency in MS Office and office management software
- Strong organizational and multitasking abilities

## Core Competencies
- Organizational efficiency and resourcefulness
- Clear written and verbal communication
- Vendor and stakeholder management
- Problem-solving under time pressure
- Confidentiality and professionalism
