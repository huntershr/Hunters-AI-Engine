# HR Generalist

## Job Brief
The HR Generalist supports a broad range of human resources functions, including recruitment, employee relations, onboarding, and policy administration. Reporting to the HR Manager, this role serves as a first point of contact for employees and helps ensure HR processes run smoothly and in compliance with labor law.

## Key Responsibilities
- Support end-to-end recruitment including job postings, screening, and interview coordination
- Manage new hire onboarding and orientation to ensure a smooth employee experience
- Serve as first point of contact for employee questions on policies and benefits
- Maintain accurate and up-to-date employee records in the HR information system
- Assist in administering performance management and employee development processes
- Support employee relations matters and escalate complex issues to HR management
- Ensure HR practices comply with Egyptian labor law and company policy

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related field
- 2–4 years of experience in a generalist HR role
- Working knowledge of Egyptian Labor Law No. 14/2025
- Proficiency in HRIS systems and MS Office

## Core Competencies
- Discretion and confidentiality
- Interpersonal and conflict-resolution skills
- Organizational and multitasking ability
- Employee-centric service mindset
- Attention to policy and compliance detail
