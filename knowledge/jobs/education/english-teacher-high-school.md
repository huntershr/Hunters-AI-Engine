# English Teacher – High School

## Job Brief
Teaches English to high-school students (about ages 14 to 18) following the school's curriculum (for example IGCSE, A-Level or an American high-school programme), building analytical reading, academic writing and language skills and preparing students for assessments.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach English language and literature lessons in line with the school's curriculum
- Teach analytical reading of novels, plays, poetry and non-fiction texts
- Teach academic and creative writing, grammar, speaking and debate
- Prepare students for internal and external exams and coursework where applicable
- Mark work, give clear feedback and track student progress
- Differentiate tasks so that all students are supported and stretched
- Contribute to department planning and share good practice
- Communicate with parents and follow the school's safeguarding policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in English, literature or a related field
- A teaching qualification (PGCE, QTS, state teaching licence or equivalent)
- At least 3 years of experience teaching English at secondary or high-school level
- Strong command of English
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
