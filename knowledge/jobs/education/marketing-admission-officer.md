# Marketing & Admission Officer

## Job Brief
Handles enquiries from prospective families and supports the school's student recruitment through marketing activity such as social media, content, open days and events, while keeping admissions records accurate.

## Key Responsibilities
- Respond to enquiries from prospective families by phone, email, social media and in person
- Guide families through the admissions process and keep application records accurate
- Organise school tours, open days and recruitment events
- Create and publish social media posts, newsletters and promotional content with the school team
- Keep the enquiry and application tracking system up to date and report on conversion
- Coordinate with school staff on assessments, placements and enrolment paperwork
- Support marketing campaigns and report on their results
- Represent the school in a professional and welcoming way

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in marketing, business, communications or a related field
- At least 1 year of experience in admissions, marketing, sales or another customer-facing role
- Strong written and spoken English, and Arabic
- Confident with social media and Microsoft Office
- Organised, friendly and professional manner with families
