# MEP Engineer

## Job Brief
The MEP Engineer designs, reviews, and supervises the installation of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems on construction projects, ensuring compliance with design specifications and codes. Reporting to the Technical Office or Project Manager, this role coordinates closely with site teams and subcontractors on MEP works.

## Key Responsibilities
- Review and develop MEP designs, drawings, and specifications for construction projects
- Supervise installation of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems to ensure compliance with design
- Coordinate with architects, structural engineers, and MEP subcontractors to resolve design clashes
- Conduct site inspections and testing of MEP systems for quality and safety compliance
- Review shop drawings and technical submittals from MEP subcontractors
- Prepare quantity take-offs and cost estimates for MEP works
- Maintain documentation of MEP installations, inspections, and commissioning records

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical or Electrical Engineering
- 3–6 years of experience in MEP design or site engineering
- Proficiency in AutoCAD, Revit MEP, and relevant design software
- Strong knowledge of Egyptian and international MEP codes and standards

## Core Competencies
- Technical precision in multidisciplinary coordination
- Problem-solving for design and site conflicts
- Attention to safety and code compliance
- Effective cross-team communication
- Quality-focused documentation
