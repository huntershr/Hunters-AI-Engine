# EYFS Homeroom Teacher (Native English Speaker)

## Job Brief
Acts as lead class teacher for a nursery or KG class (about ages 3 to 6) in an English-medium school in Egypt, planning play-based learning across the EYFS areas and helping every child feel safe, curious and confident.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach play-based activities across the EYFS areas of learning
- Teach early phonics, language, early maths and creative skills through songs, stories and hands-on activities
- Create a safe, welcoming and well-organised classroom and learning corners
- Observe each child, record progress and use it to plan next steps
- Use positive behaviour management and keep children safe and supervised at all times
- Work with co-teachers, assistants and specialist teachers to keep routines consistent
- Communicate regularly with parents through meetings, reports and daily contact
- Follow the school's child protection, health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in early childhood education, education or a related field
- A recognised teaching qualification or early-years qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching in an early-years or KG setting
- Native or near-native English; based in Egypt, as the post requires
- Patience, energy and warmth with young children and parents
