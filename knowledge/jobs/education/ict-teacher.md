# ICT Teacher (Middle & High School)

## Job Brief
Teaches ICT and computing to middle and high school students, building digital literacy, office productivity, programming basics and safe, responsible technology use, and looks after the computer lab.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and teach ICT and computing lessons in line with the school's curriculum
- Teach digital literacy and productivity tools such as documents, spreadsheets and presentations
- Introduce programming and computational thinking, for example with Scratch or Python
- Teach online safety and responsible digital citizenship
- Prepare students for assessments or external exams where the school offers them
- Look after the computer lab and report faults to the IT team
- Assess student work, give useful feedback and track progress
- Communicate with parents and follow the school's safeguarding policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in computer science, information technology or education
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching ICT or computer science at secondary level
- Strong command of English
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
