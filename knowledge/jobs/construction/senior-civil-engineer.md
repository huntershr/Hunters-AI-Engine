# Senior Civil Engineer

## Job Brief
Senior civil engineer who leads civil works or design packages on building and infrastructure projects, reviews the work of other engineers, resolves technical problems and answers for quality, cost and schedule on the civil scope.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead the execution or design of the civil scope and plan resources, sequence and methods of work
- Review drawings, calculations, shop drawings and method statements and approve or return them with comments
- Manage and mentor site engineers, supervisors and subcontractor teams on the civil works
- Prepare and check quantities, cost estimates, variation orders and subcontractor valuations
- Coordinate with the consultant, client and other disciplines and resolve technical conflicts
- Monitor progress against the programme, plan recovery actions and report to the project manager
- Make sure quality control and inspection procedures are followed and non-conformances are closed
- Enforce health, safety and environment standards and take part in risk assessments

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering
- 6–9 years of experience in civil engineering, including supervising or leading engineering teams
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and Primavera P6 or MS Project
- Strong knowledge of the Egyptian Code of Practice (such as ECP 203) and international standards such as ACI, BS or Eurocode
- Strong written and spoken English and good reporting and presentation skills
