# Call Center Agent

## Job Brief
The Call Center Agent handles inbound and outbound customer calls, resolving inquiries, processing requests, and providing accurate information in line with service standards. Reporting to the Customer Service Manager, this role is often the first point of voice contact between the company and its customers.

## Key Responsibilities
- Handle inbound and outbound calls professionally and in accordance with service scripts
- Respond to customer inquiries and resolve issues on first contact wherever possible
- Log call details, complaints, and resolutions accurately in the CRM system
- Escalate complex or unresolved issues to the appropriate department or supervisor
- Meet call quality, handle-time, and customer satisfaction targets
- Provide customers with accurate product, service, and policy information
- Follow up on pending customer requests to ensure timely resolution

## Required Qualifications
- High school diploma or equivalent; bachelor's degree preferred
- 1–2 years of experience in a call center or customer service role
- Proficiency in CRM software and basic computer applications
- Fluency in Arabic and working proficiency in English

## Core Competencies
- Clear and patient verbal communication
- Active listening and empathy
- Composure under pressure
- Accuracy in data entry and documentation
- Target-driven work ethic
