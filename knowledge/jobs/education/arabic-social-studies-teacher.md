# Arabic Social Studies Teacher (الدراسات الاجتماعية)

## Job Brief
Teaches social studies (history, geography, civics and national education) in Arabic following the Ministry of Education curriculum and the school's programme, helping students understand their country, region and world.

## Key Responsibilities
- Plan and deliver social studies lessons in Arabic that follow the Ministry of Education curriculum
- Teach history, geography, civics and national and cultural awareness
- Use maps, timelines, sources and discussion to bring lessons to life
- Develop reading, research, analysis and presentation skills
- Assess and mark student work, give useful feedback and track progress
- Support students who need extra help and stretch those who are ready for more
- Communicate regularly with parents and work with the Arabic department on planning
- Follow the school's safeguarding, health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in social studies, history, geography or education
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 2 years of experience teaching social studies at primary or preparatory level
- Strong command of Modern Standard Arabic
- Clear communication with students, parents and colleagues
