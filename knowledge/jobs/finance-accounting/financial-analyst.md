# Financial Analyst

## Job Brief
The Financial Analyst evaluates financial data, market trends, and business performance to support strategic decision-making across the organization. Working closely with the Finance Manager, this role prepares forecasts, variance analyses, and management reports that guide budgeting, investment, and cost-control decisions company-wide.

## Key Responsibilities
- Analyze financial statements, trends, and key performance indicators to support decision-making
- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial forecasts and variance analyses
- Build and maintain financial models to evaluate business scenarios and investment options
- Support the annual budgeting process and monitor actual performance against budget
- Prepare management reports and presentations summarizing financial performance and insights
- Conduct cost-benefit analysis for proposed projects and business initiatives
- Collaborate with department heads to gather data and validate financial assumptions

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or Economics
- 2–4 years of experience in financial analysis or a related field
- Advanced proficiency in Excel and financial modeling
- Strong understanding of Egyptian financial reporting standards

## Core Competencies
- Analytical and quantitative reasoning
- Attention to detail and data accuracy
- Clear financial storytelling and reporting
- Sound business judgment
- Deadline-driven work ethic
