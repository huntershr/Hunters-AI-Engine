# EYFS Co-Teacher

## Job Brief
Works alongside the lead teacher in a nursery or KG class (about ages 3 to 6), helping to plan and deliver play-based learning, supporting small groups and individual children, and keeping children safe, settled and happy.

## Key Responsibilities
- Support the lead teacher in planning and delivering play-based EYFS activities
- Lead small-group and individual activities for early literacy, numeracy and creative development
- Prepare and tidy the classroom, learning areas and resources
- Observe children, record observations and share them with the lead teacher to inform progress
- Support children's routines, personal care, behaviour and emotional well-being
- Supervise children during play, meals, arrival and dismissal
- Communicate daily with parents together with the lead teacher
- Follow the school's child protection, health and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in early childhood education, education or a related field, or a recognised early-years qualification
- At least 1 year of experience in an early-years or nursery setting
- Good spoken and written English
- Knowledge of EYFS or play-based learning
- Patience, warmth and energy when working with young children
