# German Head of Department (International School)

## Job Brief
Leads the German department of an international school, setting the curriculum direction for German as a foreign language (and as a second language where relevant), leading and mentoring the teaching team, and raising standards through assessment, moderation and results analysis.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead curriculum planning and schemes of work for German as a foreign language and, where relevant, as a second language
- Lead, support and mentor the German teaching team
- Carry out lesson observations and moderation of student work and assessments
- Oversee assessment and exam preparation, and keep marking and reporting consistent
- Analyse results, set department targets and plan improvements
- Maintain high teaching standards and share good practice across the department
- Communicate with parents and report to senior leadership on department progress
- Manage the department budget and teaching resources, and teach classes as timetabled

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree (master's degree preferred)
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 5 years of experience teaching German, including 2 or more years in a leadership or coordinator role
- Native or near-native German (C1 or higher)
- Strong leadership, organisation and communication skills
