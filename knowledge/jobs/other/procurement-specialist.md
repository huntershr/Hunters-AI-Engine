# Procurement Specialist

## Job Brief
The Procurement Specialist manages the sourcing and purchasing of goods and services, negotiating with suppliers to secure the best value while ensuring timely delivery. Reporting to the Purchasing Manager, this role supports cost control and supplier relationship management across the organization.

## Key Responsibilities
- Source suppliers and negotiate pricing, terms, and delivery schedules for goods and services
- Prepare and issue purchase orders in line with approved budgets and specifications
- Evaluate supplier performance and maintain an approved vendor list
- Monitor inventory levels and coordinate timely replenishment with relevant departments
- Ensure procurement activities comply with company policy and procurement regulations
- Resolve discrepancies related to deliveries, invoices, and purchase orders
- Prepare procurement reports and support cost-saving initiatives

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Supply Chain, or related field
- 2–4 years of experience in procurement or purchasing
- Strong negotiation and supplier management skills
- Proficiency in ERP or procurement software

## Core Competencies
- Negotiation and cost-consciousness
- Supplier relationship management
- Analytical evaluation of quotations and contracts
- Organizational and follow-up discipline
- Ethical and transparent procurement practice
