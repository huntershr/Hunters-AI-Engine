# Technical Pre-Sales Engineer

## Job Brief
Works with the sales team as the technical specialist who understands customer requirements, designs suitable solutions, runs demonstrations and proofs of concept, prepares technical proposals and helps win deals.

## Key Responsibilities
- Meet customers with the sales team to understand their business and technical requirements
- Design technical solutions that match customer requirements and available products
- Prepare technical proposals, bills of quantities, presentations and responses to tenders
- Run product demonstrations and proofs of concept
- Answer technical questions from customers and partners
- Work with vendors and internal delivery teams to confirm feasibility, scope and cost
- Hand over won projects to delivery with clear documentation
- Keep up to date with products, technologies and competitors

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in engineering, computer science, information technology or a related field
- At least 2 years of experience in pre-sales, solution design or technical consulting
- Good understanding of the technologies or products the company sells
- Strong presentation skills and written English
- Customer-focused and able to explain technical topics simply
