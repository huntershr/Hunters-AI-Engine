# Head of Physical Education (PE Head of Department)

## Job Brief
Leads the physical education department of a school, setting the PE curriculum, leading and mentoring PE teachers and coaches, running the sports programme and events, and keeping all sports activity and facilities safe.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead curriculum planning and schemes of work for physical education
- Lead, support and mentor PE teachers and coaches
- Organise sports days, inter-house events, fixtures and tournaments
- Make sure sports facilities and equipment are safe, maintained and used correctly
- Oversee assessment of students' skills and fitness and keep reporting consistent
- Promote participation, fair play and healthy lifestyles across the school
- Communicate with parents and report to senior leadership on department progress
- Teach classes as timetabled and follow the school's safeguarding and safety policies

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in physical education, sports science or education
- A recognised teaching qualification
- At least 5 years of experience teaching physical education, including 2 or more years in a leadership or coordinator role
- Valid first aid certificate, or the ability to obtain one before starting
- Strong leadership, organisation and communication skills
